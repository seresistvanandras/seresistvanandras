Cryptography · Security · Privacy

Research at the intersection of cryptography and cryptocurrencies.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Princeton University, supervised by Prof. Matt Weinberg. My research focuses on the security and privacy of cryptocurrencies. I especially enjoy practically motivated problems that lead to new cryptographic ideas.

I completed my PhD in Computer Science at Eötvös Loránd University (ELTE) in 2023. Before Princeton, I was a researcher at ELTE and completed research internships at IMDEA Software Institute and a16z crypto.
